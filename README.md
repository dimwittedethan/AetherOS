= AetherOS

AetherOS is an experimental, independent Linux distribution built and maintained by one person. Development is an ongoing project, and progress may sometimes be slow due to school, personal commitments, and the amount of work involved in maintaining an entire distro.

⚠️ Experimental Software

AetherOS is still under development. You may encounter:

Bugs and unexpected behaviour
Missing or unfinished features
Breaking changes between updates
Hardware compatibility issues
Installation problems
Features that are changed or removed

Do not use AetherOS as your only operating system if you rely on your computer for important work or data. Always keep backups of anything important.

🐛 Reporting Issues

Issues, bug reports, feature requests and suggestions are welcome! If you find something wrong, please open an issue on GitHub with as much useful information as possible.

When reporting a bug, try to include:

What happened
What you expected to happen
Steps to reproduce the problem
Your AetherOS version
Relevant logs or error messages
Hardware information where relevant

However, please don't expect an immediate response or fix. AetherOS is a one-person project, meaning development, testing, packaging, documentation, support and issue management are all handled by me.

Some issues may take days, weeks, or potentially longer to investigate and fix. Some suggestions may also never be implemented depending on their complexity, usefulness, or compatibility with the project's direction.

🧪 Use at Your Own Risk

AetherOS is provided as an experimental project. Installing or using it can potentially result in data loss, installation failures, broken configurations, or in extreme cases an unusable system.

You are responsible for your own system and your own data.

I am not responsible for:

Lost or corrupted data
Broken installations
Hardware damage
System instability
Failed updates
Bricked systems

Please make backups before experimenting with AetherOS.

🚧 Development

AetherOS is developed in my spare time. School and real-life responsibilities take priority, so there will be periods where development is more active and periods where updates may slow down significantly.

There is no large development team behind AetherOS — it's just one person building, testing and maintaining the project.

Thanks for checking out AetherOS and, if you decide to try it, thank you for being part of the project!
